Edit your carName, body, style and paint. Plate, model and VIN come from Honda and stay read-only.

1 · HomeTap the car name.
or Settings → VehicleThe first row opens the same screen.
2 · Your carSwipe body, slide style and paint, rename. Each change saves at once.
Rules

No Save button. A change saves when the finger lifts (swipe, scrub) or the name field loses focus. One "Saved" toast; a new change replaces it.

The preview, Home, widgets and Siri use the new name and look at once. Empty name falls back to Honda's model name.

Photo style uses /car/image. If Honda has no photo, the option shows the drawing and a line: "No photo from Honda".

Reset puts body, style, paint and name back to what Honda reports. The toast says "Reset to Honda's details".

Multi-car later: the car name on Home becomes the car switcher; editing moves into each car's row.

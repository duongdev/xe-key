Rules
PlaceNo second tab. The trip list under the summary card becomes the day list for the period on the card (Week / Month: days; Year: months). Honda's Day/Month/Year switch and calendar = our card switch + pager + bar tap.
Day rowRing (acc, 2.5 pt) with the trip count, day ("Sat 1 Nov" / "T7, 1/11"), "3 trips", total km and total time (short form), chevron. 60 pt min. All days start open; tap a header to close / reopen (state kept for the session). No haptic (it is navigation).
Trips in a dayIndented under the day. Places saved: small time range on top (10 pt, same grey as the stat labels), then from / to; no places: start / end time are the two lines, no extra time line. km pill and minutes on the right, aligned to the from line. Under it one metrics row in 3 equal columns (Max speed, Avg speed, Fuel) so the bars line up from row to row; every value carries its unit (km/h, km/l). Each trip's metrics row has its own small labels right above it (10 pt, 2 pt gap): Max speed · Avg speed · Fuel. Tap → Trip detail (PIN rules unchanged).
Speed bandsHonda's bars are speed bands, not scores: 1 segment ≤ 49 km/h, 2 for 50–89, 3 for ≥ 90 (Max column from max speed, Avg from avg). Filled acc for band 1, warn for 2–3; empty `line`. Fuel uses a different shape on purpose: a 24 pt continuous meter (fill 6→16 km/l) + value in km/l, coloured against this car's own 30-day average: 10%+ worse = warn (thirsty), within ±10% = fg2, 10%+ better = acc (thrifty). Relative, because a fair km/l differs per car. VoiceOver reads one sentence per trip with the numbers, not the bars.
Bar tapTapping a day bar on the card scrolls that day under the card and opens it if closed; other days stay as they are. Replaces the earlier "Sat 31 Oct · 2 trips / Show recent" header; the list header now reads the period ("Last week · 5 days").
YearRows are months (ring = trips that month). Tap → the card switches to Month on that month; the list shows its days.
DataWeek / Month: /car/trip?day= for each driven day in the period (no PIN), totals from the trips. Year: /car/trip/summary?summaryType=Month rows. Days with no trips are skipped.
MotionOpen / close animates the height (grid rows 0fr ↔ 1fr / Reanimated measured height, Settle 280 ms) with the trips fading 200 ms and the chevron turning 90°. Rows below move with it, never jump. Reduce Motion: no height animation, 150 ms fade.
No driftThe card never moves. Opening a day only pushes rows below it.
